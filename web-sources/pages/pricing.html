Eight things that shape your moving cost

{{icon:route}}

Distance

How far your belongings travel — across town, across the province or across the country.

{{icon:home}}

Property size

The number of rooms sets the crew size, truck space and time needed.

{{icon:box}}

Volume of belongings

Two homes of the same size can hold very different amounts. Your inventory helps us plan accurately.

{{icon:calendar}}

Moving date

Summer months, weekends and month-end dates are in higher demand than mid-week, off-season dates.

{{icon:truck}}

Services

Loading, unloading and furniture delivery can be added or left out to suit your plans.

{{icon:hands}}

Packing

Full or partial packing and unpacking, including fragile items and kitchenware.

{{icon:warehouse}}

Storage

Short- or long-term storage when your move-out and move-in dates don’t line up.

{{icon:shield}}

Special handling

Pianos, artwork, antiques, heavy safes and difficult access like narrow stairs or long carries.

Pricing FAQ

Is the online estimate my final price?{{icon:plus}}

No. The online estimate is a price range based on the information you provide. A RealMovingCanada representative reviews every request and confirms the final price with you before your move.

How is distance calculated?{{icon:plus}}

We estimate the road distance between the cities you enter. Exact addresses, access and routing are confirmed when a representative reviews your quote.

Why does my moving date change the estimate?{{icon:plus}}

Demand is highest in summer, on weekends and around the end of the month. Choosing a mid-week or off-season date can lower your cost.

Do I need an account to get an estimate?{{icon:plus}}

No — the instant estimate and the formal quote request are both open to everyone. No account or sign-up is required; a representative will follow up directly by phone or email.

What counts as special handling?{{icon:plus}}

Items that need extra equipment, crew or care — such as pianos, artwork, antiques and very heavy items — and difficult access such as narrow stairwells or long walks to the truck. Mention them in your request so we can plan for them.

Can I change details after requesting a quote?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Contact us with the change and a representative will update your quote.
